# Diarrhea

## Overview
Diarrhea involves frequent loose or watery stools. A major concern is loss of fluids and electrolytes, which can lead to dehydration.

## Common Symptoms
- Loose or watery stools
- Abdominal discomfort or cramps
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Weakness

## Questions to Ask
- How long has the diarrhea been present?
- How frequently are stools occurring?
- Is there blood in the stool?
- Is there vomiting?
- Can the person drink and keep fluids down?
- Are there signs of dehydration?
- Is there severe abdominal pain?

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent medical attention if there is:
- Severe dehydration
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe confusion
- Blood in stool
- Severe abdominal pain
- Persistent vomiting preventing fluid intake
- Rapidly worsening symptoms

## General Guidance
When safe and possible, maintaining fluid intake is important. Persistent, severe, or concerning diarrhea should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

## Safety
Do not independently diagnose the cause or recommend prescription medication.
