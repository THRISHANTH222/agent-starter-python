# Headache

## Overview
Headaches are common and can have many causes. The assistant should identify unusual or severe symptoms that may require urgent medical assessment.

## Common Symptoms
- Head pain
- Pressure or throbbing sensation
- Sensitivity to light or sound
- Nausea

## Questions to Ask
- When did the headache start?
- Is it sudden or gradual?
- How severe is it?
- Is this different from previous headaches?
- Is there fever?
- Is there vomiting?
- Is there weakness, numbness, confusion, or difficulty speaking?
- Was there a recent head injury?

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent medical attention if there is:
- Sudden extremely severe headache
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe confusion
- Weakness or numbness
- Difficulty speaking
- Seizure
- Headache following a serious injury
- Rapidly worsening symptoms

## General Guidance
The assistant should provide general information and recommend professional evaluation when warning signs, severe symptoms, or unusual changes are present.

## Safety
Do not diagnose the cause of a headache through the voice interaction.
