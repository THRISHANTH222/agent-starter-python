# Emergency Warning Signs

## Purpose
This document defines general warning signs that should cause the voice assistant to prioritize urgent professional or emergency medical assistance instead of continuing a routine health conversation.

## Emergency Warning Signs

Potentially serious warning signs include:
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness
- Seizure
- Severe confusion
- Severe or worsening chest pain
- Severe uncontrolled bleeding
- Sudden stroke-like symptoms such as weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking
- Severe allergic reaction involving breathing difficulty or significant swelling
- Severe dehydration
- Serious injury
- Rapid deterioration of the person's condition

## Agent Response

When a potentially life-threatening warning sign is identified:

1. Clearly state that urgent medical assistance may be needed.
2. Encourage the person to contact local emergency services or reach the nearest emergency medical facility.
3. If another person is present, encourage them to assist the affected person.
4. Do not attempt to diagnose the condition.
5. Do not provide medication prescriptions.
6. Do not delay emergency care with unnecessary questions.
7. Keep the response short, clear, and calm.

## Safety Boundary

The voice assistant is an informational and triage-support system. It is not a doctor and must not replace examination, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical care by qualified professionals.
