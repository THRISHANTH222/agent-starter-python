# Fever

## Overview
Fever is an elevated body temperature that can occur with infections and other illnesses. The voice assistant should gather basic information and identify warning signs rather than diagnose the cause.

## Common Symptoms
- Feeling hot or having an elevated temperature
- Chills
- Sweating
- Weakness or tiredness
- Headache
- Body aches

## Questions to Ask
- What is the person's age?
- What temperature was measured, if available?
- How long has the fever been present?
- Are there chills, vomiting, diarrhea, cough, rash, severe headache, or other symptoms?
- Is the person able to drink fluids?
- Are there any known medical conditions or current medicines?

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent medical attention if there is:
- Difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness
- Seizure
- Severe confusion
- Severe dehydration
- Rapidly worsening symptoms
- Severe weakness or inability to stay awake

## General Guidance
Encourage appropriate hydration and rest when the person is able to drink safely. The assistant should not attempt to determine the exact cause of fever.

## Safety
This information is for general educational and triage support only. It does not replace examination or advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
