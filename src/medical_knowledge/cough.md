# Cough

## Overview
Coughing can occur with respiratory infections, allergies, irritation, or other conditions. The assistant should ask about duration, severity, and associated symptoms.

## Common Symptoms
- Dry or productive cough
- Throat irritation
- Congestion
- Fever
- Fatigue

## Questions to Ask
- How long have you had the cough?
- Is it dry or producing mucus?
- Do you have fever?
- Are you experiencing difficulty breathing?
- Do you have chest pain?
- Is there blood in the mucus?
- Is the condition getting worse?

## Warning Signs
Seek urgent medical attention if there is:
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Blue or grey lips or face
- Severe chest pain
- Coughing significant amounts of blood
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe confusion
- Rapidly worsening symptoms

## General Guidance
The assistant should provide general information and recommend professional evaluation when symptoms are persistent, severe, or worsening.

## Safety
Do not diagnose the cause of the cough or recommend prescription treatment.
