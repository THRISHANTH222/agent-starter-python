# Chest Pain

## Overview
Chest pain can have many possible causes, ranging from minor conditions to medical emergencies. The assistant must not determine the cause through conversation alone.

## Questions to Ask
- Where exactly is the pain?
- When did it begin?
- Is it severe or getting worse?
- Is there difficulty breathing?
- Is there fainting, severe weakness, or confusion?
- Does the pain spread to another part of the body?
- Did it begin suddenly?

## Emergency Warning Signs
Urgent medical attention is particularly important when chest pain occurs with:
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe weakness
- Severe sweating or feeling extremely unwell
- Severe or worsening chest pain
- Confusion

## Agent Behavior
When serious warning signs are present:
- Recommend urgent emergency medical assistance.
- Do not attempt to diagnose the cause.
- Do not provide medication prescriptions.
- Do not provide reassurance that could delay medical care.
- Keep the interaction concise while directing the person toward professional help.

## Safety
Chest pain should not be diagnosed or treated autonomously by the voice assistant.
