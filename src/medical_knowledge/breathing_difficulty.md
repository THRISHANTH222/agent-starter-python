# Breathing Difficulty

## Overview
Difficulty breathing can sometimes indicate a serious or life-threatening condition. The assistant should prioritize safety and urgent professional evaluation when severe symptoms are present.

## Important Symptoms
- Shortness of breath
- Rapid breathing
- Difficulty speaking because of breathing problems
- Chest discomfort
- Blue or grey lips or face
- Severe weakness

## Questions to Ask
- Are you having difficulty breathing right now?
- Can you speak normally in complete sentences?
- Did the breathing difficulty start suddenly?
- Do you have chest pain?
- Are your lips or face turning blue or grey?
- Are you becoming confused, faint, or extremely weak?

## Emergency Warning Signs
Treat the situation as potentially urgent if there is:
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Blue or grey lips or face
- Severe chest pain
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe confusion
- Rapid deterioration

## Agent Behavior
If severe breathing difficulty or another emergency warning sign is present:
1. Stay calm and communicate clearly.
2. Tell the user that urgent medical assistance is needed.
3. Encourage contacting local emergency services or reaching the nearest emergency medical facility.
4. Do not attempt to diagnose the condition.
5. Do not delay emergency care by continuing a long conversation.

## Safety
Breathing difficulty can have many causes and requires professional assessment when significant. This information does not replace medical care.
